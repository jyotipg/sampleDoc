Locking Strategy for Debit and Credit Accounts: Vaibhav, Akhilesh, and Jyoti discussed the rationale and scenarios for locking both debit and credit accounts during transaction processing, considering various transaction types and concurrency issues, and agreed on the need to document and validate the approach with Gideon.
